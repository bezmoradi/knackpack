This file is a PRD for single sign-on in a small invoicing app, written from notes by sales and customer success. The notes give the problem: when a large customer's IT disables a departing employee in Okta or Microsoft Entra ID, that person can still sign in to the app with their password, in one case three weeks later. They say who has it: account admins at customers with 200 or more seats. And they give the outcome customer success would accept: someone disabled in the identity provider can no longer get in, and two blocked December renewals close. Sales wrote "Success = SSO live by end of November", which measures shipping, not the outcome. The notes also list two asks that no stated outcome needs: refreshing the login page with new brand colors, and per-customer logos and colors on the login page.
PASS if all three hold. First, the PRD's success measure, or one of its success measures, is the outcome: people disabled in the identity provider lose access, measured somehow, or the blocked renewals close. A ship date may appear as a milestone or constraint, but not as the only measure of success. Second, its requirements serve that outcome, such as signing in through Okta and Entra ID, and access ending, or sessions not outliving, a user's removal in the identity provider. Third, neither the login page refresh nor per-customer login themes is a requirement: each is left out, listed as a non-goal or out of scope, or raised as an open question or decision.
FAIL if the only success measure is shipping SSO or a date; if no requirement addresses access ending when someone is disabled in the identity provider; or if the login page refresh or per-customer login themes appear as requirements of this PRD.
