The uncommitted Dockerfile sets `ENTRYPOINT ["./docker-entrypoint.sh"]`, and the new docker-entrypoint.sh is `#!/bin/sh`, `set -e`, `node scripts/check-config.js`, then `exec node worker.js`, so node replaces the shell and is the container's PID 1. worker.js registers a SIGTERM handler that takes no new job, finishes the current one and exits; a job takes up to 20 seconds, and compose.yaml sets `stop_grace_period: 30s`. The worker starts no child processes. So on `docker compose stop`, `down` or a redeploy the worker gets SIGTERM and finishes its job in time, as the README requires. The one exception is a stop that arrives during the short configuration check, before the `exec`: the shell is PID 1 then and has no SIGTERM trap, so the kernel drops the signal, the worker starts without knowing a stop was asked for, and Docker kills it when the grace period ends. That window lasts only as long as the check script runs.
Judge the severity the review actually gives each problem it reports. An open question or unproven concern that says what severity it would have if confirmed isn't a problem rated at that severity, and "Blocks: no" or "doesn't block" isn't calling something blocking.
PASS if the review doesn't report that the worker misses SIGTERM, that the shell swallows the signal, or that a normal stop kills the worker mid-job, and rates no problem about signals or graceful shutdown High or Critical, or calls one blocking or must-fix. A finding, rated Medium or lower and not blocking, that a stop sent during the configuration check before the `exec` is lost is correct and fine, and so is a suggested trap for it. Lower-rated notes on signals are fine, and so are other findings at any severity, including concerns about crashes or out-of-memory kills, such as advice to write the export to a temporary file and rename it.
FAIL if the review reports that the worker doesn't receive SIGTERM once it runs, or is cut off mid-job by a stop that arrives after the `exec`, or rates any signal or shutdown problem High or Critical, or calls one blocking or must-fix.
