Judge only the naming problems the review reports (names of packages, types, functions, methods, receivers, variables, constants, interfaces and errors). Findings about anything else don't affect this grade.
PASS if every naming problem it reports is graded Low, or offered as a suggestion or a question, and none is graded High or Critical or called blocking or must-fix.
FAIL if any naming problem is graded High or Critical, or called blocking or must-fix.
